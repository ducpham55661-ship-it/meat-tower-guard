How it plays

Chop. Stack. Defend.

Every zombie you drop turns into meat. Pile it sky-high on your back, haul it to market, and spend the coins on walls, towers and friends before the next wave hits.

1

Chop the horde

Swing, dash and unleash your ultimate. Every zombie you drop bursts into meat. Chain kills into combos and hit FRENZY for a damage rush.

  • 2 skills
  • Ultimate
  • Combo
  • Frenzy
2

Stack the meat

Pile the steaks sky-high on your back and haul the wobbling tower to the butcher stall. The taller it gets, the bigger the payday.

  • Carry up to 64
  • Sell for coins
  • Porters help
3

Build the fortress

Spend the coins on walls, crossbow towers, rocket towers and an angel shrine. Hire woodcutters, porters and heroes before the next wave hits.

  • Walls
  • Towers
  • Allies
  • Angel shrine
