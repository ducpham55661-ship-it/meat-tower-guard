How it plays

Chop. Stack. Defend.

Every zombie you drop turns into meat. Pile it sky-high on your back, haul it to market, and spend the coins on walls, towers and friends before the next wave hits.

1

Chop the horde

Swing, dash and unleash your ultimate. Chain kills into combos and hit FRENZY for a damage rush.

2

Stack the meat

Balance a wobbling tower of steaks. The taller the tower, the bigger the payday at the butcher stall.

3

Build the fortress

Raise walls, crossbow towers, rocket towers and an angel shrine. Hire woodcutters, porters and heroes.
